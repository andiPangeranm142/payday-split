Reactive contract · running on-chain

Every payday, your crypto splits itself.

Deposit ETH once. A reactive contract on Reactive Lasna sees it, computes your split, and routes the money — savings locked away, a slice swapped to stablecoin, the rest stays spendable. Set the rules once; the chain does the rest, forever.
